# Privacy Policy – n8n Google Integrations

**Last Updated: October 17, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how our private n8n automation workflows access and process Google account data through Google APIs.

The integration is intended for internal automation and is not offered as a public application.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted, our workflows may access:

- Google Sheets spreadsheets and related data.
- Google Drive files and folders necessary for automation tasks.
- Basic Google account information required for authentication.

## 3. How We Use Information

Google data is used to perform authorized automation tasks, including reading, creating, updating, and organizing spreadsheet records and files.

Access is limited to the functionality required by each configured workflow.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Data may be processed through our self-hosted n8n environment and stored in the connected Google account.

We do not sell Google user data or share it with third parties for advertising purposes. Data may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the integrations.

## 5. Data Security

We use access controls and OAuth authentication to help protect Google account access. Access is restricted to authorized operators.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained according to the operational needs of the relevant workflows. Authorized users may request deletion of data under our control.

## 7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
